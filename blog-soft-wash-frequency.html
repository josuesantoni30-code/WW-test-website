How Often Should You Soft Wash Your Home in Texas?

📅 2026  ·  ⏱ 5 min read  ·  By Wicked Washers CTX

If you live in Waco, Texas or anywhere in Central Texas, you've probably noticed your home's exterior starts looking dingy, streaky, or green well before you expected it to. The heat, humidity, and year-round outdoor moisture create near-perfect conditions for algae, mold, and mildew to thrive on siding, brick, stucco, and roofs.

The question we hear most from Waco homeowners: "How often do I actually need to get my house soft washed?"

The honest answer: it depends — but we can give you solid guidelines based on what we see working on hundreds of homes across the Waco area every year.

The General Rule for Central Texas

For most homes in Waco and the surrounding area, we recommend a professional soft wash every 18–24 months. That's the sweet spot where you're keeping biological growth controlled before it causes damage, without over-treating surfaces unnecessarily.

Compared to other parts of the country, Central Texas warrants more frequent cleaning than, say, the desert Southwest — but less than humid coastal areas like Houston or Florida. Waco's combination of summer heat, periodic heavy rain, and warm winters creates conditions where algae and mildew regrow faster than they would in drier climates.

Factors That Change the Frequency

The 18–24 month guideline is a starting point, but several factors can push your home toward more frequent treatment:

1. Shade and Tree Coverage

Shaded surfaces dry more slowly after rain, giving algae and mildew more time to establish. Homes with heavy tree coverage on the north or east side — the sides that get the least direct sun — often need soft washing every 12–18 months. If you notice green or dark staining concentrated on shaded walls, you're in this category.

2. Proximity to Water

Homes near Waco's lakes, the Brazos River, or areas with natural drainage and moisture hold more airborne algae spores. If your property sits in a low area or next to a water feature, plan on annual or 18-month intervals.

3. Roof Condition

A roof covered in algae or moss is constantly shedding spores onto your home's walls. If your roof hasn't been cleaned, it becomes a source of ongoing recontamination for your siding. Our recommendation: clean the roof and the exterior walls in the same service cycle so you're not fighting recontamination from above.

4. Siding Material

Some materials attract biological growth faster than others:

  • Vinyl siding — highly resistant but not immune. Every 18–24 months is typical.
  • Wood siding — absorbs moisture and grows algae faster. Annual treatment often needed.
  • Stucco and EIFS — textured surface traps moisture. Often needs treatment every 12–18 months.
  • Brick — durable but can harbor mildew in mortar joints. Every 2–3 years typically.
  • Hardie board — similar to vinyl. 18–24 months is standard.

5. History of Delayed Cleaning

If it's been 3+ years since your last professional exterior clean, expect to see heavier growth — and potentially some early paint or surface damage. Homes that have been neglected for extended periods may need two treatments spaced a few months apart to fully address deeply rooted biological growth.

Signs Your Home Is Overdue for Soft Washing

  • Black, green, or brown streaks on siding or walls
  • Green tint on north or east-facing walls
  • White chalky areas on vinyl or painted surfaces (oxidation)
  • Dark staining near downspouts and gutters
  • Visible mildew in corners or under eaves
  • Your neighbors' houses look noticeably cleaner than yours

Why Waiting Too Long Costs More

Algae and mold are not just cosmetic problems. Over time, biological growth does real damage:

  • Paint failure — algae breaks down paint binders, causing premature fading and peeling
  • Wood rot — moisture held by organic growth accelerates wood decay on trim and siding
  • Stucco cracking — moisture intrusion from established mold can degrade stucco and EIFS systems
  • Shingle damage — on the roof, algae and moss trap moisture against shingles, shortening their lifespan significantly

The cost of a professional soft wash in Waco is a fraction of a paint job, wood repair, or roof replacement. Regular cleaning is maintenance — not a luxury.
