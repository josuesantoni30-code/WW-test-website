Why Your Fleet's Appearance Matters

🚛 First Impressions on Every Job

Your trucks arrive at customers' properties before your team does. A clean fleet says "professional." A dirty one raises doubts before the work even starts.

🔧 Vehicle Longevity

Road salt, grime, and organic debris accelerate corrosion on frames, undercarriages, and painted surfaces. Regular washing extends fleet lifespan and reduces maintenance costs.

👷 Driver Pride

Drivers who operate clean vehicles report higher job satisfaction. A clean truck is a cared-for truck — and it shows your team you take pride in the operation.
