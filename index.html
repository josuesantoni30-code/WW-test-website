Soft washing uses low water pressure combined with biodegradable cleaning solutions to safely remove mold, mildew, algae, and grime from your home's exterior, roof, and siding. Unlike high-pressure washing, soft washing will not damage paint, shingles, mortar, or delicate surfaces — and the results last 4–6× longer. It's the gold standard for home and roof washing in Waco, TX.

Pressure washing uses high water pressure to physically blast away dirt — great for concrete, driveways, and hardscapes. Soft washing uses low pressure plus specialized cleaning agents to safely treat and remove organic growth (mold, algae, mildew) from surfaces like roofs, siding, and painted wood where high pressure could cause damage.
