What We Clean

Everything From Glass
to Tracks to Sills

Window cleaning isn't just wiping glass. A complete professional job includes every component of the window system — because dirty tracks and grimy sills ruin the look of clean glass.

  • 🪟
    Exterior Glass — squeegee technique or water-fed pole (WFP) for upper floors. Streak-free finish guaranteed.
  • 🏠
    Interior Glass — careful interior access with drop cloths. No drips, no mess.
  • 🔲
    Screens — removed, cleaned, re-installed. Debris and oxidation removed from frames.
  • 🧹
    Tracks & Sills — vacuumed and detail-cleaned. Dirt and debris that collect in tracks are fully removed.
  • 🏢
    Storefronts & Commercial Glass — scheduled maintenance programs available for retail, office, and commercial properties.
💡
Water-Fed Pole (WFP) System: For upper floors, we use a water-fed pole with 0 TDS purified water — no ladders needed, no streaks as it dries. The gold standard for multi-story window cleaning.

Benefits

Why Clean Windows Actually Matter

☀️

More Natural Light

Dirty windows block up to 40% of natural light. Clean windows make every room brighter, more inviting, and reduce energy costs by maximizing daylight.

🏢

Professional First Impressions

For commercial properties, windows are the face of your business. Clean windows signal professionalism and care — dirty ones do the opposite.

📈

Increased Property Value

Clean, well-maintained windows are a strong curb appeal signal. For homes on the market, professional window cleaning is one of the highest-ROI pre-sale improvements.

🛡️

Extends Window Life

Hard water deposits, oxidation, and mineral buildup can etch and permanently damage glass over time. Regular cleaning prevents the permanent damage that requires window replacement.

🌿

Healthier Indoor Environment

Window tracks and sills harbor allergens, mold spores, and debris. A complete window cleaning removes these and improves indoor air quality — especially important in Waco's allergy season.

🔒

100% Streak-Free Guarantee

If you find a streak after we leave, call us. We come back and fix it. No arguments, no extra charge. That's the Wicked Washers promise on every window job.
