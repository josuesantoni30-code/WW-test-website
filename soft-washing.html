What Is Soft Washing?

The Smart Way to
Clean Your Home

Soft washing is a professional cleaning method that replaces high-pressure water with a low-pressure application of biodegradable cleaning solutions. Instead of blasting dirt off your home with force, soft washing kills the biological growth at the root — algae, mold, mildew, and bacteria — so it doesn't grow back for years.

The result is a deeper, longer-lasting clean that won't void your roof warranty, damage your siding, or strip your paint — something pressure washing at high PSI absolutely can do.

💡
Soft wash vs. pressure wash: Pressure washing uses force to remove surface dirt. Soft washing uses chemistry to kill the organisms causing the staining. For homes, roofs, and siding, soft washing is the professional standard — it's safer, more effective, and results last dramatically longer.

What Soft Washing Removes:

  • ✓ Gloeocapsa Magma algae (black streaks)
  • ✓ Mold and mildew growth
  • ✓ Lichen and moss
  • ✓ Green and brown oxidation stains
  • ✓ Pollen, dust, and atmospheric fallout
  • ✓ Bird droppings and organic stains

Our Process

What to Expect on
Your Soft Wash Day

A professional soft wash takes 2–4 hours for an average home. Here's exactly what we do:

1

Pre-Job Walkthrough & Property Protection

We walk the property with you, identify any concerns, cover outdoor furniture and sensitive plants, and pre-wet all landscaping within reach of chemical overspray. Windows and doors are confirmed closed.

2

Pre-Wet All Vegetation

Before applying any cleaning solution, we thoroughly saturate all grass, shrubs, flower beds, and vegetation near the work area. Saturated plants absorb dramatically less chemical.

3

Apply Soft Wash Solution

We apply a precisely mixed sodium hypochlorite + surfactant solution at low pressure, working top to bottom. The surfactant helps the mix cling to vertical surfaces and extend dwell time so the chemistry can work.

4

Dwell Time

The solution dwells on the surface for 5–15 minutes, killing biological growth at the root. We keep surfaces wet during dwell — never letting the mix dry on the surface.

5

Low-Pressure Rinse

After dwell, we rinse thoroughly with clean water at low pressure — removing the cleaning solution and dead biological matter from the surface.

6

Final Plant Rinse & Walkthrough

After the rinse cycle, we thoroughly water all vegetation again, remove all property protection, and walk the job with you to confirm the result. Your property is left clean — no mess, no residue.
