What We Pressure Wash

Concrete & Hardscape
Cleaning Done Right

Not every surface should be pressure washed at the same PSI — and not every surface should be pressure washed at all. Our team knows the difference. We use the correct pressure, nozzle, and technique for each surface to maximize cleaning without causing damage.

  • 💥Driveways & Parking Areas — concrete, asphalt, pavers. Oil pre-treatment included.
  • 💥Sidewalks & Walkways — residential and commercial foot traffic areas.
  • 💥Patios & Outdoor Living — concrete, stamped, brick pavers, pool decks.
  • 💥Retaining Walls & Brick — careful inspection of mortar condition before wash.
  • 💥Commercial Storefronts & Entries — first impressions matter. We keep them clean.
  • 💥Garage Floors — degreaser pre-treatment + high-pressure finish.
