Why Soft Wash — Not Pressure Wash

Pressure Washing Your Roof
Voids the Warranty

Asphalt shingles have a protective granule coating that gives them weather resistance, UV protection, and color. High-pressure water strips those granules instantly — and most shingle manufacturers explicitly state that pressure washing voids your warranty.

Soft washing applies a cleaning solution at near-zero pressure. The chemistry does the work, killing algae, moss, and lichen at the root. Rain gradually washes away the dead material over the following weeks.

⚠️
Never pressure wash asphalt shingles. It permanently strips granules, dramatically shortens roof life, and voids manufacturer warranties. Soft wash only — always.
💡
The black streaks you see on roofs are caused by Gloeocapsa Magma — an airborne algae. It's present on almost every roof in Central Texas and grows in humid conditions. Soft washing kills it completely.

Our Process

How We Soft Wash Your Roof

1

Ground Inspection & Quote Confirmation

We assess the roof from the ground and from a ladder — identifying growth type, pitch, and any structural concerns. We confirm scope before any work begins.

2

Cover & Pre-Wet All Vegetation

We cover sensitive plants near the foundation with tarps, pre-wet all landscaping within 20 feet, and set up continuous rinsing for surrounding vegetation during treatment.

3

Apply Soft Wash Solution Bottom to Top

Using a 12V pump system, we apply the SH + surfactant mix starting at the bottom edge and working upward — preventing untreated chemical from running under already-treated areas.

4

Full Dwell Time

We allow 15–25 minute dwell time depending on growth type. The solution must stay wet — we'll re-apply if drying begins. Do not rush this step.

5

Gentle Low-Pressure Rinse

After dwell, we rinse with low pressure — just enough to remove chemical residue. The goal is not to blast off the dead growth; rain will handle that over the coming weeks.

6

Final Vegetation Rinse & Walkthrough

We thoroughly rinse all landscaping and walk the completed job with you. Expect continued improvement over the next 2–4 weeks as rain removes residual dead material.
